General Mechanics

Automatic Membership: All OKBet account members are automatically enrolled in the VIP Program upon registration.
How to Rank Up: Members can progress to higher VIP levels by accumulating Growth Values through bets on eligible products: Sports, Egames, Perya, and Live Dealer Games.
Rewards & Bonuses: Reaching a new VIP level unlocks corresponding rewards and bonuses. The Upgrade Bonus can be claimed once per account for every new VIP level achieved.
VIP Level Retention: Once a member reaches a VIP level, the level will be retained without monthly turnover-based downgrades.
System Governance: All Growth Value thresholds, VIP level configurations, bonus amounts, and regression speed parameters are governed by automated system settings.

Upgrade Requirements

Members can reach higher VIP levels by accumulating the required Growth Values through eligible betting activities.
Upon meeting the required Growth Value, members will qualify for the corresponding VIP level.
VIP level upgrades will be processed on the following day after 3:50 AM.
Growth Values are updated daily starting at 3:50 AM onwards.
Members who successfully reach a new VIP level will receive the corresponding Upgrade Bonus.

VIP Level Retention

Members will retain their achieved VIP level without monthly turnover-based downgrades.
No monthly VIP Retain requirements will be imposed.
Failure to meet a higher VIP level's Growth Value requirement will not result in a deduction or downgrade of the member's existing VIP level.
Members can continue accumulating Growth Values to qualify for higher VIP levels.

Terms and Conditions

The promotion applies to Perya, Slots, Casino, Sports, Arcade, Poker and Numeric games.
Rewards earned are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for other items or credits.
